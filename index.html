Tomato Early Blight

Scientific: Alternaria solani

Fungal Infection Treatable Seasonal: Summer
warning Symptoms Observed
  • Concentric rings in leaf spots
  • Yellow chlorotic halo around lesions
  • Lower leaf defoliation
healing Treatment Plan

Organic Correction

Remove infected lower leaves and apply copper-based fungicide or Neem oil solution every 7-14 days.

Cultural Practice

Increase spacing between plants for better airflow and avoid overhead watering to keep foliage dry.
